Praise Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Praise is an offline-first lyrics app. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

Local data

Praise stores songs, favorites, lists, custom lyrics, and saved song photos in local app storage on your device.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo access are used only when you choose to scan a song or keep a song photo. Saved photos remain in local app storage unless you share or delete them.

OCR and on-device AI

OCR runs on the device where supported. On supported Android devices, OCR text may be passed to Android on-device AI APIs to help structure a song draft. You review the draft before saving it.

Network use

Praise uses network access for:

  • Refreshing the public song catalogue
  • Opening YouTube practice videos
  • Opening shared list links
  • Submitting feedback, problem reports, or song requests

Feedback and song requests are sent to the Praise support workflow for review. Please avoid including private information in those submissions.

Community catalogue

Catalogue songs are community-provided. Praise does not intend to use anyone's work without permission. If your work appears in the catalogue without consent, please report the song so it can be reviewed for removal.

Accounts

Praise does not require a user account for offline lyrics, favorites, lists, custom songs, or local scan features.

Contact and removal requests

To report an issue, request a song removal, or ask a privacy question, use the report option inside the app or contact the app publisher.
